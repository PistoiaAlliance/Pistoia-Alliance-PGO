<summary><b>Comments</b></summary>
<!-- ## Comments -->

* Participants agreed that the term 'Cell' should refer to the highest-level concept, distinct from its more specific derivatives ('cell type' and 'cell line'), each of which warrants its own dedicated definition in future PGO phases. The NCIT_C12508 definition was selected based on frequency of use, foundational biological scope, and its ability to accommodate sub-concepts through the planned LinkML framework.
* Recommendation to the PGO Steering Group. 1. Adopt NCIT_C12508 as the reference definition for 'cell' in the PGO. 2. Consider the separate definition of 'cell type' and 'cell line' as distinct, core concepts in subsequent ontology development phases.
